## Requested asset delivery

1. The actual Runway narration MP3 (prefer the original downloaded file). No replacement speech will be generated and no unnatural time compression applied.
2. A cinematic electronic music track or music stem with promotional and paid-advertising rights. Supply source/license information. Preferred creative texture: warm, spacious, clear, with restrained transients and a clean resolve; no constant buzz, harsh distortion or abrasive sustained high tones. Tempo and final beat alignment follow the chosen track, not a fabricated placeholder score.
3. An approved external SFX stem, or a small licensed effects pack that can be conformed to this sheet. Useful families: dry UI clicks, magnetic resolves, smooth air passes, subtle material/glass accents and soft low impacts. Avoid excessive whooshes or coin/casino associations.

Descript can provide the cleaned voice and final mixed 48 kHz WAV once available. An approved premixed stem can replace the separate delivery tracks for final integration. Do not add music beneath the narration MP3 itself unless intentionally delivering that approved mix.

## Mixing and verification targets

Inspect narration duration, head/tail silence, phrase boundaries and peaks first. Keep natural speed. If a phrase cannot fit the visual windows, propose the smallest script change before generating a replacement. Protect the final words of the reported 29.6-second recording; do not assume a 0.4-second margin until measured.

Use gentle corrective dialogue EQ and compression only as needed; duck music around words, soften SFX that compete with speech, and use controlled fades and limiting. Start around -16 LUFS stereo with a -1.5 dBTP ceiling as delivery targets, then assess intelligibility and the encoded AAC result. They are not measured results for missing audio. No final master should be exported before the real voice and intended soundtrack have been reviewed together.
