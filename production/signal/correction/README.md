## Limits relevant to the reference target

This is a corrective review, not a claim of four-reference quality equivalence or final creative approval. The film uses real Blender-authored mesh assets but its animated pixels are rendered by Three.js, not Cycles. Screen-space transmission, thickness-based refraction and shader dispersion approximate the optical transport. The designed split beam is composited; no path-traced spectral caustics, physically simulated beam splitting, optical manufacturing tolerances or true topology-changing glass fusion are claimed. Convergence uses continuous vertex deformation and interlocking composition.

Sceneplane's current free deployment limits renders to 1024 pixels per side, 128 samples and 300 seconds per job, with raw scripting disabled. No low-resolution Blender animation was upscaled into this film. Reflection highlights remain deliberately strong; human review should judge their finish, the type pacing and the match to the reference standard in full motion.
